## Why it wins, criterion by criterion

1. **It relaxes:** the pacing itself slows the reader — short quiet sentences, a house settling, a distant sound fading; nothing in it hurries or demands.
2. **Calm from the text itself:** it only shows — a ticking house, a train in the dark. No imperatives, no "breathe", no instruction to relax, nothing forced.
3. **Craft:** plain concrete words (wood, train, fields), a slow three-beat rhythm, and "ticks once, twice" — a sound that paces the reading; no word could be cut.
4. **It dwarfs:** the train crossing the dark and fading into the fields opens a vast quiet landscape that keeps growing after the last line.
5. **Freshness:** its own images — a settling house, wood ticking, a far-off night train — no stock wellness phrases, no reworded meditation script.
